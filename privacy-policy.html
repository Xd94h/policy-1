01Who we are

Shivam Mobile Shop (“we”, “us”) is a mobile phone shop and customer service point at Kalyan Road, Bhiwandi Bypass, in front of Patel Residency, Shop No. 06, Bhiwandi, Maharashtra. For the personal information described here, we decide why and how it is used.

This policy applies to information we receive in the shop, by phone or WhatsApp, and through our website. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

03How we collect it

  • Directly from you when you hand over documents, fill in a form, speak to our staff or pay at the counter.
  • Through messages when you send photos or documents on WhatsApp or by email.
  • From the service process, such as an acknowledgement number, application status or transaction reference sent back by a government portal, operator or partner.
  • From our shop systems, such as billing records and CCTV.

04How we use it

  • To carry out the service you asked for and to tell you its status.
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud, forgery and misuse of our services.
  • To issue bills and receipts, and to keep accounts and tax records.
  • To handle warranty claims, returns, complaints and support.
  • To follow the law, regulator instructions and partner requirements.
  • To contact you about your own request. We will send promotional messages only if you agree to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

We do not use your information for any purpose that is not connected to the above, and we do not sell, rent or trade it.

05Consent and legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you ask for a service and hand over your information. We may also process it where the law allows or requires, for example to meet KYC rules, to answer a lawful order, or to maintain records.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (Section 15). Withdrawal does not affect what we have already done, and we may not be able to continue a service without the information it needs. We may also have to keep certain records where the law requires it.

We handle your data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, as they apply to us.

06Who we share it with

We share your information only when it is necessary for your service or required by law:

  • Authorities and portals: the government department or portal that handles your Aadhaar, PAN, PF or similar application.
  • Operators and partners: telecom operators for SIM services, and transport booking platforms for tickets.
  • Money transfer partners: the licensed provider, banks and payout partners in India and Nepal who process your transfer.
  • Payment services: UPI apps and banks that process your payment.
  • Law and regulators: courts, police and regulators when we receive a valid legal request, or to report suspected fraud or unlawful activity.
  • Service providers: technicians or professionals who help us run the shop, under a duty to keep your information confidential.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. These recipients handle your data under their own policies, and we are not responsible for how they manage it after it leaves us.

07Aadhaar, PAN and biometrics

Aadhaar, PAN and biometric details are especially sensitive, so we handle them with extra care.

  • We ask for your Aadhaar number or e-Aadhaar only when a service truly needs it, and we use a masked Aadhaar where that is accepted.
  • Biometric data is captured only on the authorised device for the specific service you requested. We do not keep copies of your fingerprints or iris scans on our own systems.
  • OTPs are for one-time use for your service only. Our staff will not use an OTP for any other purpose.
  • Photos, scans and PDFs of your documents are kept only as long as needed to complete your request and meet record-keeping rules, as described in Section 08.
  • We will not ask you to share your Aadhaar, PAN or OTP for lotteries, prizes or offers. If someone claiming to be us does, please do not respond and tell us.

08Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including:

  • Restricting access to our staff who need it to serve you.
  • Protecting our computers and phones with passwords and keeping them updated.
  • Keeping paper documents in a secure place and returning originals to you after use.
  • Deleting temporary scans, photos and downloads from our devices once the service is complete.

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or for as long as the law requires for accounts, tax and KYC records. After that, we delete or anonymise it. Typical periods are:

09CCTV at the shop

We use CCTV cameras in and around the shop for the safety of customers, staff and property, and to help resolve disputes and prevent theft or fraud. Footage is viewed only by authorised persons, kept for a limited period, and shared only with the police or authorities when lawfully requested or when needed to investigate an incident.

10Our website

Our website is an information page. It does not ask you to log in, and we do not run advertising trackers on it. Like any website, the hosting provider may automatically record technical data such as IP address, browser type and visit time for security and operation.

If you contact us through a link on the site, such as a phone, WhatsApp or email link, your message is handled under this policy. Our site may link to other websites, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Access: tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct: fix information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete: erase your information when it is no longer needed or when you withdraw consent, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent: stop us from using your information for any purpose that depends on your consent.
  • Complain: raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the relevant authority such as the Data Protection Board of India, once it is operational under the law.

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 15. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

12Children

We provide services for a person under 18 only with the consent and presence of a parent or legal guardian, who must provide their own valid ID. We do not knowingly collect a child’s information without that consent, and we do not use it for tracking, profiling or marketing.

13Data breaches

If we become aware of a breach that affects your personal information, we will take steps to contain it and will inform you and the relevant authority as required by law.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The latest version, with its effective date, will be displayed at the shop and on our website. Where a change is significant, we will make a reasonable effort to let you know.
